Avocado Juice


learn more

Ingredients:

2 ripe avocadoes
Milk
Sugar
Description:
Avocado juice is a creamy, decadent beverage made by blending ripe avocado flesh with a liquid like milk or water, and a sweetener such as sugar, honey, or condensed milk.
Making:
In a blender, mix the milk, sugar and the chopped avocadoes. blend until a watery consistency is achieved. serve while cold with salty snacks
