Ingredients:

Description:
Milk tea is a versatile beverage made by blending brewed tea with milk, sweeteners, and sometimes chewy toppings like tapioca pearls.
Cooking:,
In a sufuria put milk,tea leaves and sugar to your taste, let it boil for 15 mins serve while hot with salt or sweet snacks accompaniments.

Avocado Juice


learn more

Ingredients:

2 ripe avocadoes
Milk
Sugar

Description:
Avocado juice is a creamy, decadent beverage made by blending ripe avocado flesh with a liquid like milk or water, and a sweetener such as sugar, honey, or condensed milk.
Making:
In a blender, mix the milk, sugar and the chopped avocadoes. blend until a watery consistency is achieved. serve while cold with salty snacks
